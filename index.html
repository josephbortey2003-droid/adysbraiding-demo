About Ady's

Ady's is a neighbourhood African braiding salon at 1736 Dorchester Ave. We braid every day of the week, and walk-ins are always welcome.

From knotless and boho to Fulani, cornrows and kids' styles, every look is done by hand with neat parts and gentle tension.
